Project Idea:

MAL3ABY is a smart sports court booking platform that brings searching, comparing, booking, and payment together in one place. Users can choose their preferred sport - Football, Tennis, or Padel - and set their location to find the nearest available courts. They can easily compare courts based on prices, available times, distance, ratings, photos, and court condition before making a booking.

Project Overview

The Problem

Currently, booking a sports court can require users to:

  • Search for courts through Google Maps or social media.
  • Contact court owners through phone calls or WhatsApp.
  • Ask about available times and prices.
  • Check several courts separately to compare.
  • Confirm availability before going to the court.
This process can be time-consuming, inconvenient, and difficult to compare.

Our Solution

MAL3ABY brings the entire process into one organized platform.

Instead of searching across different platforms and contacting multiple court owners, users can find nearby courts, compare their options, choose an available time, and complete their booking online.

Project Objectives

Simplify Sports Field Booking

Provide users with a fast, easy, and organized way to search for and book football, tennis, and padel courts online.

Help Users Find the Best Nearby Fields

Use location-based services to show users the nearest available sports fields and help them compare locations, prices, ratings, and available time slots.

Provide a Convenient Online Booking & Payment Experience

Allow users to check real-time availability, select their preferred time, confirm their booking, and pay online securely.

Create an All-in-One Sports Platform

Integrate an online sports store where users can purchase sports equipment, clothing, and accessories alongside their field reservations.

Improve Sports Facility Management

Help field owners manage their facilities, schedules, bookings, prices, and customer information through an organized management system.

Increase Accessibility to Sports Activities

Make it easier for individuals and groups to discover, compare, and book suitable sports facilities based on their location, budget, and preferred time.

Project Scope

Scope 01

Sports Field Booking System

Develop an online system for booking football, tennis, and padel courts, including available time slots and booking confirmation.

Scope 02

Location-Based Field Search

Allow users to find nearby sports fields based on their current location, with filtering by sport, distance, price, rating, and availability.

Scope 03

Online Payment System

Provide secure online payment options that allow users to complete their bookings quickly and conveniently.

Scope 04

Sports Equipment & Clothing Store

Integrate an e-commerce section where users can browse and purchase sports equipment, clothing, shoes, and accessories.

Scope 05

Field Management & User Dashboard

Provide dashboards for users and sports facility owners to manage bookings, schedules, prices, orders, and other relevant information.

Final Deliverable

A complete web-based sports platform that allows users to:

Search and book football, tennis, and padel courts across different sports clubs and facilities.
Find nearby available sports facilities using location-based search.
Compare prices, availability, ratings, and previous user reviews before booking.
Make individual or group bookings and invite friends to join a game.
Create or join sports teams and find players for different sports activities.
Complete online booking and secure payment through the platform.
Access offers, discounts, and special promotions from sports facilities.
Browse and purchase sports equipment, clothing, shoes, and accessories through the integrated sports store.
Explore additional services available at clubs, such as cafés and recreational activities.
Manage bookings, orders, teams, friends, reviews, and personal information through a dedicated user dashboard.
Provide sports facility owners with a management dashboard to manage courts, schedules, bookings, prices, offers, and customer information.

The final product will be a user-friendly, responsive, and integrated sports platform that combines sports field booking, team formation, social features, online payments, and sports e-commerce in one place.
